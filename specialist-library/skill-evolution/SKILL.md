# Skill Evolution

Capture a redacted learning event with source/revision, scope, correction and outcome. Distinguish one-project behavior from a general preference. Search existing lessons and counterexamples. Propose a small diff; compare unchanged baseline and candidate on the same model, task, tool scope and repository. Use held-out regressions and human teaching/design judgments, repeated trials where variance matters. Record correctness, explanation quality, time and tokens when available. Do not silently modify global rules or auto-promote instincts. After human approval, version the change and retain rollback. The kit eval cases provide starting fixtures, not proof of improvement.
